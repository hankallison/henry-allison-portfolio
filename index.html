About
Add photo

Henry Allison is a film producer and editor based in Salt Lake City, Utah. His work spans documentary, branded content, and narrative film — always in service of stories that matter.

He is the co-producer of Noah Kahan: Out of Body, a feature-length documentary about the musician Noah Kahan, coming to Netflix on April 13, 2025. The film follows Kahan through a pivotal period in his career, capturing the making of music that has resonated with millions of people around the world.

Henry is a partner at Brown Barn Studios, a boutique documentary production company that specializes in helping businesses and organizations demonstrate their impact. The company's work is grounded in a belief that documentary storytelling is one of the most powerful tools available for communicating what an organization stands for and why it matters.

He also writes on Substack about filmmaking, storytelling, and the creative process.
